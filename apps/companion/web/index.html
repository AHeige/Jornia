      01 / Journal

      Connect your WoW folder

      Jornia detects common WoW installation folders. Confirm your client folder to discover all character directories and import every existing Jornia journal.

      Install the addon from the ZIP’s AddOns folder. It asks separately before recording. WoW saves at /reload or logout; other addon contents and account settings are not read. Folder-only characters have no gear or journey until a source supplies it.

      Journal reading is off.

      Paste an addon export

      Run /wisp export in WoW, then paste the packet here.

      History & privacy

      Journals and conversations stay on this computer. AI questions go to Jornia and Berget. Journal excerpts are shared only with your permission for each question. Avoid sensitive information in questions.

      Jornia does not encrypt its local database. Protect your computer account and backups.

      02 / Your character

      Character portrait & gear

      Fetch a public Blizzard Forever profile. Your character’s full name and region go to Jornia and Blizzard; portrait images load from Blizzard. No journal is sent.

      Dated Armory data, not live equipment. Refresh manually; cached profile data expires after 28 days. No Blizzard login needed.
