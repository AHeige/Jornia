# Jornia visual assets — night expedition

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-11-06, then copied into this repository. No CLI generation or provider key was used. The PNG avatar retains transparency; the UI version was resized to 512px with sips. The landscape was converted to JPEG for distribution. No Blizzard art was generated or copied into these assets.

- Original avatar: `docs/design/assets/wisp-original.png`
- Runtime avatar: `apps/companion/web/media/wisp.png`
- Runtime landscape: `apps/companion/web/media/expedition.jpg`
- Download-page copies: `apps/download/media/wisp.png` and `expedition.jpg`
- Small SVG mark: an original compass/star in the HTML; the old droplet is removed.

The actual player render is requested from Blizzard's render host at runtime. It is not a bundled asset and is labelled separately from the concept scenery.

## Avatar prompt

Create a single original game companion mascot asset for Jornia, a personal fantasy RPG travel journal. A small floating ROUND magical lantern spirit: squat round faceted midnight-blue metal/stone body, a warm amber star-shaped light core, two small attentive pale ice-blue eyes, two detached tiny brass rune shards orbiting its sides and a thin broken bronze compass ring. Charming and curious, mature painterly fantasy-game inventory icon quality, tactile hand-painted material, strong compact readable silhouette even at 48px. Full body centered with breathing room on a genuinely transparent background. Palette deep navy, steel blue, warm amber, subtle ivory light. Absolutely no teardrop or flame body, no hanging tail, no green leaf, no legs, no human body, no text, no lettering, no logos, no surrounding scene, no icon border. Not photoreal, not glossy mobile-game candy, not anime. This is the actual UI avatar asset, not a mockup.

## Landscape prompt

Original wide cinematic hand-painted fantasy RPG landscape illustration for a character journey journal called Wisp. Midnight expedition, a rocky overlook and winding mountain trail above an immense misty cobalt-blue valley, distant weathered citadel and aqueduct silhouettes, indigo night sky with a few pale stars and a thin moon. Warm small amber campfire at the lower right beside an open travel journal and a humble canvas bedroll, very faint embers, no people, no humanoid figures, no characters. Aged brass compass rests beside journal. Strong illustrated terrain shapes with painterly game-environment material, atmospheric depth, crisp composed foreground fading into the blue horizon. Palette ink navy, slate blue, pale icy moonlight, restrained molten amber at camp. Left third is dark open sky and mist reserved for UI text; main environmental landmarks on the right. Wide landscape 16:9 composition, sophisticated fantasy exploration mood, adventurous not horror. No text, no lettering, no UI, no logos, no neon cyberpunk, no green forest, no ornamental border, no watermark. This will be a real website/application background asset, not a UI mockup.
